# Supervised Research Project – Non-Newtonian Fluids

**Academic Year: 2023–2024**

Experimental research project investigating the properties of **non-Newtonian fluids** and their potential applications in bicycle helmet impact protection.

## Project Overview

This project was conducted during the 2023–2024 academic year as part of the **TIPE (Travaux d'Initiative Personnelle Encadrés)**, a supervised research project undertaken in French preparatory classes for engineering schools.

The research focused on understanding how non-Newtonian fluids respond to mechanical stress and exploring their potential use in impact protection systems.

The main objective was to investigate whether their mechanical properties could contribute to improving bicycle helmet safety.

## Methodology

The project combined experimental investigation with computational data analysis:

- **Literature Review:** Studied the behavior and properties of non-Newtonian fluids.
- **Experimental Investigation:** Explored the response of non-Newtonian materials under different experimental conditions.
- **Data Analysis:** Processed experimental measurements using Python.
- **Data Visualization:** Generated graphs to analyze and interpret experimental results.
- **Scientific Communication:** Documented the research methodology and presented the findings.

## Technologies & Skills

- **Programming:** Python
- **Scientific Computing:** Experimental data processing and visualization
- **Research Methods:** Experimental design, scientific analysis and interpretation
- **Scientific Communication:** Technical documentation and oral presentation
